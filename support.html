LabTempo Support

Help with the LabTempo app for iPhone.

Email labtempo@protonmail.com

The quickest way is Settings, Support, Send feedback in the app: it fills in the app version for you. For anything about credits, include the device ID shown in Settings.

A protocol parsed badly

Tell me which model you used and, if you can, paste the text. A parse that comes back unusable is refunded, up to five a day.

Moving to a new phone

  • Your protocols and runs: Settings, Backup, Back up everything to a file. Get the file to the new phone, then Settings, Backup, Restore a backup.
  • Your credits: Settings, AI, Move to new phone gives a one-time code. On the new phone, Settings, AI, Enter a code.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using your own AI key

Settings, AI lets you add a key from Mistral, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI or DeepSeek. Your phone then talks to that provider directly and no credits are used.

Privacy

Your work stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
