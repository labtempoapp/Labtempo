LabTempo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

LabTempo turns laboratory protocols into timed, runnable steps. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data. The short version: your work stays on your phone, and there is no account.

In plain terms. Your protocols, run records, photos and notes are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us. We have no account system, so we cannot see your work. The only time protocol text leaves your phone is when you ask the app to parse it with AI. When that happens through LabTempo credits, it is not used to train any AI model, because we run on paid commercial API accounts where the providers commit not to train on content. The detail is below.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your phone and never sent to us:

  • Your protocols, including every step, reagent, buffer and timing
  • Your run history, deviation notes, catalogue and lot numbers
  • Photos you take of results
  • Calendar entries, scheduled runs and reminders
  • Your API keys, if you supply your own, which are held in the operating system's encrypted keychain

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. You can also erase everything at any time using "Delete everything on this device" in Settings.

The one exception: AI parsing

To convert a written protocol into steps, its text must be sent to an AI model. There are two ways this can happen, and you choose which.

Using your own API key. The protocol text is sent directly from your phone to the AI provider you selected (such as Anthropic, Google, Mistral, OpenAI or DeepSeek). It does not pass through us. Your relationship is with that provider, under their terms and privacy policy. We never see the text and never see your key.

Using LabTempo credits. The protocol text is sent to the LabTempo service, which forwards it to the AI provider using our key, counts the credits used, and returns the result to your phone. We do not store the protocol text. It is held only in memory for the moment it takes to complete the request.

What the LabTempo service does store

If you use LabTempo credits, our server keeps two things:

  • An anonymous device identifier and the credit balance attached to it, so your credits follow you between sessions. This identifier is generated randomly on your device. It is not linked to your name, email address, Apple ID, or any other identity.
  • A record of purchases (the store transaction identifier and how many credits it granted), so that a purchase cannot be redeemed twice.

We also record, for each parse, which AI model was used and how many tokens it consumed. This is used only to keep the credit prices accurate. It does not include the protocol text or anything about you.

How the AI providers handle your protocols

This is the question researchers ask first, so here is the accurate answer rather than a reassuring one.

If you use LabTempo credits, your protocols are not used to train any AI model. LabTempo pays for commercial API accounts with Anthropic, Google and Mistral. On these paid API accounts, all three providers commit in their terms that content is not used to train their models. That commitment is the reason this app runs on paid accounts rather than free tiers, where the terms are different and content often is used for training.

Your protocol is sent, parsed, and returned. It is not added to a training set. It is not reviewed by us. It is not kept by us.

Retention. The providers do hold content briefly. Typically inputs and outputs are retained for around 30 days for abuse monitoring and legal compliance, then deleted. This is a safety measure on their side, not a training pipeline, and it is the honest limit of what we can promise. Nobody retains your protocols indefinitely.

Unpublished work. We built this for bench scientists, and we know what an unpublished method is worth. That is exactly why the managed service runs on paid API accounts with no-training terms. If your protocol is confidential, that is the safest path we can offer short of not sending it anywhere at all.

Their current terms are the source of truth, not this page:

  • Anthropic: is my data used for model training?
  • Google: Gemini API Additional Terms of Service
  • Mistral: Privacy Policy

If you bring your own key. The text goes straight from your phone to the provider you chose, and we never see it. But check which plan your key is on. Several providers treat free and paid tiers differently, and a free-tier key may permit training on your content. If that matters to you, use a paid key, or use LabTempo credits where we have already handled it.

A word of judgement. The no-training commitments above are made by those companies, not by us, and terms change. If you are working with genuinely sensitive material, read their current terms and decide for yourself. We would rather you make an informed choice than take our word for it.

What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts, logins or passwords
  • We do not collect your name, email or contact details
  • We do not use analytics or tracking of any kind
  • We do not serve advertising
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone
  • We do not store your protocol text

Payments

Credit packs are purchased through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Those companies handle the payment and receive your payment details. We never see your card details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, which we use to add credits to your device identifier.

Children

LabTempo is intended for researchers, students and professionals working in laboratories. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your control

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing personal to request or correct. You can remove everything held on your device at any time through Settings. If you wish to have the anonymous credit record associated with your device identifier deleted, contact us at the address below and include the identifier, which is shown in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
