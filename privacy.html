LabTempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Contact: labtempo@protonmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • Your protocols, runs, photos, cell lines and everything else you record stay on your phone.
  • If you buy credits, our service stores a random device ID, your credit balance, your purchase records and a count of today's failed parses. It does not store your protocols.
  • A protocol you choose to parse is sent to an AI provider. With your own API key it goes straight there. With credits it passes through our service, which forwards it and keeps nothing.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. We do not sell or share your data.

Data stored only on your phone

LabTempo saves the following in the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

  • Protocols, folders and recipes.
  • Runs and past runs: notes, logged changes, step times, ratings, catalogue and lot numbers, samples, gels and plates.
  • Photos you take or attach.
  • Cell lines and passage logs, storage boxes, primer pairs, projects, to-dos and calendar entries.
  • Your settings.

API keys and ELN tokens (eLabFTW, Notion) are stored in the iOS keychain. They are only sent to the service they belong to.

“Delete everything” in Settings removes this data from your phone. Deleting the app does the same, except that iOS keeps keychain items, so your device ID (see below) survives a reinstall and your credits come back.

Your phone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) include LabTempo's data according to your Apple settings. A backup file you export from LabTempo is yours. It goes wherever you send it.

AI parsing

AI is optional. Writing a protocol yourself, importing one and the tutorial work without it.

When you paste text, attach a file or describe an experiment and ask LabTempo to turn it into steps, that content is sent to an AI model. There are two ways this happens.

With your own API key. The content goes directly from your phone to the provider you chose: Anthropic, Google, Mistral, OpenAI or DeepSeek. It does not pass through us. Your use is governed by your agreement with that provider.

With LabTempo credits. The content goes from your phone to our service, which forwards it to Anthropic, Google or Mistral and returns the result. Our service does not store or log the content. These providers process it under their commercial API terms. Under those terms they do not use API content to train models and they may keep it for a limited period to monitor abuse.

What our service stores

Our service runs on Cloudflare. It only stores data when you use credits.

  • Device ID. A random identifier made on your phone the first time you use credits. It is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID. You can see it in Settings.
  • Credit balance, and when it last changed.
  • Purchases. For each credit pack: the App Store transaction ID, your device ID, the number of credits granted, the time, and whether Apple has refunded it. This is how a purchase is redeemed exactly once.
  • Usage totals. For each parse: the model, the number of tokens in and out, the credits charged, and the time. These records do not contain your device ID or any of your content.
  • Failed parses today. How many unusable replies your device ID has had today, so refunds can be limited. Earlier days are deleted.

Purchases

Apple processes all payments. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID. When you buy credits, the app sends us the transaction ID and Apple's signed transaction record. We check it with Apple's App Store Server API before adding credits. If Apple refunds a purchase, Apple tells our service, which checks with Apple and removes that pack's credits. If they were already used, your balance goes below zero until your next purchase.

Other services the app contacts

These are contacted only when you use the feature.

  • Paper search. Your search terms and article identifiers go to Europe PMC, run by EMBL-EBI. To look for a free copy of a paper, its DOI goes to Unpaywall.
  • Links. When you paste a link (a recipe or kit page) or open a link from a QR code, the app fetches or opens that page directly. That website sees your IP address, as with any browser.
  • ELN export. When you send a run to eLabFTW or Notion, it goes to that service with your token. Sending to Overleaf opens a new Overleaf project containing your methods text.

Camera, photos and notifications

  • The camera is used to take photos you choose to keep and to read QR codes. QR codes are read on the phone. Camera frames are not saved.
  • Photos you take or attach are copied into the app's storage. They are included in exports you make.
  • Timer, reminder and culture notifications, and Lock Screen timers, are created on your phone. They are not sent from a server.

Where data goes

Cloudflare, Anthropic, Google and OpenAI are based in the United States. Mistral is based in France. DeepSeek is based in China, and is only used if you add your own DeepSeek key. Where our service sends data outside the EU, it relies on the safeguards these companies provide.

Legal basis

We process your device ID, balance and purchase records to provide the credits you bought (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We keep purchase records to stop a purchase being redeemed twice or kept after a refund, and count failed parses to stop refunds being abused (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

Your device ID and balance are kept while you can still use your credits. Purchase records are kept for as long as the purchase could be redeemed again. The count of failed parses is kept for the current day only. You can ask us to delete them at any time. Deleting them also deletes your remaining credits.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about your device ID, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email labtempo@protonmail.com with the device ID shown in Settings. We have no other way to link the data to you.

Children

LabTempo is made for laboratory researchers and is not directed at children.
